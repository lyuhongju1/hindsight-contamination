## What this pool suggests for the real search

- **Collider HEP dominates.** The 2008 LHC detector papers supply about 6 post-baseline items on their own, so the field caps in the selection rules (§4) will bind there.
- **Strongest-looking for a pilot:** T2K ND280 → SuperFGD (#8), and AugerPrime (#11). Both are distinctive, clearly post-baseline, and outside the companion study's fields.
- **Ambiguous dating cases** (#7, #10, #12, #17) are useful **dating-protocol rehearsals** for the two daters before the real search.
- **Thin fields.** Double-beta decay and gravitational waves supply few post-baseline items, so these fields may fall short of the target.
- **Not usable as primary items:** #18 is contemporaneous, and #17 is borderline. Use them, if at all, as examples of exclusions.
