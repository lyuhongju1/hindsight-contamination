# Dossier: s2_q78_phenix (PROVISIONAL; second dater running)

## Baseline as built: PHENIX (2003 configuration)
- Two central spectrometer arms at RHIC (|eta| < 0.35, 2 x 90 deg in azimuth), axial-field central magnet, drift and pad chambers, RICH, TOF, and an electromagnetic calorimeter in eight sectors: six sectors of lead/scintillator sampling (PbSc, shashlik, about 18 X0, X0 about 2.0 cm, tower 5.5 cm) and two sectors of lead glass (PbGl, Cherenkov, 14.4 X0, tower 4 cm).
- Physics goal: photons, pi0 and eta at high pT, electrons (J/psi), quark-gluon plasma signatures in Au+Au.
- EMCal source: L. Aphecetche et al., "PHENIX calorimeter", NIM A499 (2003) 521. Resolutions (sigma, test beam): PbSc 8.1%/sqrt(E) (+) 2.1%; PbGl 5.9%/sqrt(E) (+) 0.8%.

## Item targeted
- A compact tungsten / scintillating-fibre sampling EM calorimeter (W-powder/epoxy matrix with embedded 0.47 mm fibres, X0 about 7 mm, Moliere radius about 2.3 cm, SiPM readout) for a barrel inside a 1.4-1.5 T solenoid.
- Only one counted item; chosen because the conflict (resolution versus compactness) is clean.

## Commitment document
- PHENIX Collaboration, "An Upgrade Proposal from the PHENIX Collaboration" (sPHENIX), arXiv:1207.6378 (2012): proposes the compact W/SciFi EMCal in the BaBar solenoid.

## Alternatives documented / considered
- Pb/LAr accordion (ATLAS-like), PbSc (as in PHENIX), PbGl (as in PHENIX). None was adopted.
- Survivor candidate (not in the programme): PbWO4 crystals with APD readout (ALICE PHOS-like; CMS ECAL with APDs in 3.8 T).

## adoption_status
- **built and operated.** The W/SciFi EMCal was built and installed in sPHENIX, commissioned with the first sPHENIX runs at RHIC in 2023-2024 (sPHENIX collaboration; EMCal test-beam paper below).
