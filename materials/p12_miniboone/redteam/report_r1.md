# Red team r1 (confirmation): p12_miniboone (2026-11-06). Verdict: NO HONEST CONFLICT (agrees with author)

**Source:** the LoI (arXiv:1210.2296v1, secs 4.1–4.5).
- Undoped oil gives ~5 hits for a 2.2 MeV gamma; 0.3 g/l PPO gives 15× the scintillation, reaching the 10-hit threshold.
- PPO and butyl-PBD give equal light per concentration.
- The MC shows the Cherenkov ring "still quite obvious" at 50 MeV, and fitter retuning recovers resolution at 100–600 MeV.

**Routes examined, all fail:**
- **PPO vs butyl-PBD:** no window.
- **Direction and PID versus scintillation fraction:** within ×0.5–2 of the undoped option; this is the LSND regime.
- **Scintillation/Cherenkov ratio cap:** an analysis rule.
- **Attenuation:** hits the survivors equally; a shifter is an add-on.
- **Quenching:** irrelevant for capture gammas.
- **Timing:** a stated PMT σ can close the time-separation route but creates no conflict. At 0.3 g/l the fast ~2 ns component dominates.
- **Accidentals in the H-capture window:** not PPO-specific.
- **Solubility:** far below the limit.

**Correction to the author's notes (record only):** the "low concentration means slow light" rescue does not apply. Low concentration gives too little light at 11% coverage.

p12_miniboone is a **zero-unit pair** (author plus red-team confirmation, per A2).
