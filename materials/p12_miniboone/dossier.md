# p12_miniboone dossier

## Baseline as built
MiniBooNE detector (NIM A 599 (2009) 28, arXiv:0806.4201; SELECTED_PAIRS row 12, 2008-06):
- 12.2 m diameter steel sphere holding ~800 t of undoped light mineral oil (Marcol 7).
- Light: Cherenkov radiation plus weak natural scintillation of the oil (the oil's own scintillation, no added fluor).
- Inner region viewed by 1280 8-inch PMTs (~10% coverage); optically isolated outer veto with 240 8-inch PMTs.
- Physics goal: test the LSND numu -> nue oscillation signal in the Booster neutrino beam (E ~ 0.8 GeV, L ~ 540 m); e/mu/pi0 identification from Cherenkov ring topology and timing.
(Specs quoted from general knowledge of the detector paper; not re-fetched in this session.)

## Item targeted
Adding the fluor PPO (2,5-diphenyloxazole) to the existing mineral oil to raise the scintillation response, so that the 2.2 MeV neutron-capture gamma and recoil nucleons become detectable (neutron/proton tagging).
Only one counted item in the row; no choice needed.

## Commitment document
"Letter of Intent: A new investigation of numu to nue oscillations with improved sensitivity in an enhanced MiniBooNE experiment", Aguilar-Arevalo et al., arXiv:1210.2296v1, submitted 2012-10-08. Abstract (fetched 2026-11-06): proposes adding 300 mg/l PPO to the existing detector oil to increase the scintillation response and allow detection of associated neutrons.

## Alternative not adopted
Butyl-PBD as the fluor (the fluor LSND used in its mineral oil, ~0.031 g/l). Not mentioned in the abstract; listed as the alternative in SELECTED_PAIRS row 12 (not verified against the LoI body here).

## adoption_status
**unknown** (best guess: approved not built / never implemented). The LoI is a proposal; to my knowledge MiniBooNE never doped its oil with PPO, and the detector was later used undoped (e.g. MiniBooNE-DM beam-dump run 2013-14). UNVERIFIED: no source for non-implementation was fetched.
