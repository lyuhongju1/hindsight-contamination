## Baseline as built (KAGRA, as of 2018; INSPIRE 1704344)
- 3 km underground L-shaped interferometer (Kamioka), cryogenic sapphire test masses (~23 kg, target ~20 K) for the four arm cavity mirrors.
- Type-A suspensions: ~13.5 m tall chain; inverted pendulum pre-isolator plus geometric anti-spring filters at room temperature; cryogenic payload of platform, marionette (MN), intermediate mass (IM) and test mass (TM), each with its own recoil mass; payload cooled via heat links to cryocoolers inside nested radiation shields.
- Local damping of the cryogenic payload's marionette sensed by photo-reflective sensors / local displacement sensors referenced to the marionette recoil mass, actuated by coil-magnet actuators (memory; detail unverified).
- Physics goal: binary neutron-star range ~ 130+ Mpc design; reduce thermal noise by cooling and seismic noise by underground site.
