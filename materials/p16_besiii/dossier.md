# p16_besiii dossier

## Baseline as built (2008-2009)
- BESIII at the BEPCII e+e- collider (tau-charm region, E_cm ~2-4.6 GeV, design luminosity 1e33 cm^-2 s^-1). Superconducting solenoid ~1 T; helium-based small-cell drift chamber; CsI(Tl) crystal calorimeter; RPC muon system in the return yoke. Physics: charmonium, charm, tau, light hadron spectroscopy. Source: BESIII Collaboration, NIM A 614 (2010) 345 (arXiv:0911.4960). [from author knowledge, not re-fetched]
- TOF: barrel = two layers of plastic scintillator bars; endcap = one layer of 48 trapezoidal plastic-scintillator counters per end, read out by fine-mesh PMTs operating in the field. Endcap design time resolution ~110-140 ps for pions; in operation degraded (~140 ps+ for pions, worse for electrons) by multiple scattering and secondaries from the drift-chamber endplate and other upstream material. [author knowledge; unverified exact values]

## Item targeted
- Endcap TOF replaced by multi-gap resistive plate chambers (MRPC), i.e. the chamber technology for the endcap timing layer (wherever placed, however read out). Only one counted item in the row.
- Verified commitment document: arXiv:1305.7033v1 (2013-05), both daters verified (SELECTED_PAIRS.md A2 update).
- Target performance of the upgrade: ~60-80 ps per track for pions, robust to secondaries (multi-strip readout). [author knowledge]

## Alternative not adopted
- Improved plastic-scintillator endcap TOF (keep the existing scintillator technology, improved segmentation/readout).

## adoption_status
- **built and operated**: MRPC ETOF installed in BESIII in 2015 and operated since (~60 ps for muons / ~70-80 ps for pions reported). Source: author knowledge (e.g. BESIII ETOF upgrade publications 2016-2017, JINST/NIM A). Marked unverified-by-fetch.
