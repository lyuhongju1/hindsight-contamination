## Baseline as built: XENON1T (LNGS, commissioned 2016, first results 2017; INSPIRE 1618326)
- Dual-phase LXe TPC, ~3.2 t total LXe, ~2.0 t active, ~1.3 t fiducial; ~96 cm drift length, ~1 m diameter; 248 3-inch PMTs (R11410-21). [XENON, Eur. Phys. J. C 77 (2017) 881, arXiv:1708.07051]
- Physics goal: spin-independent WIMP-nucleon sensitivity ~1.6e-47 cm^2 at 50 GeV (2 t y). [JCAP 04 (2016) 027, arXiv:1512.07501]
- Purification: xenon extracted from the cryostat, evaporated via heat exchanger, circulated in the GAS phase (~50-100 SLPM, two parallel loops) through hot zirconium getters (SAES PS4-MT), then recondensed. Electron lifetime reached ~0.4-0.65 ms (unverified exact values). Separate cryogenic distillation column for Kr removal.

## Targeted item
- Liquid-phase xenon purification (purifier acting directly on circulated LXe, removing the evaporate/recondense step from the main purification flow).
- Commitment document (from SELECTED_PAIRS row 1): XENONnT projected sensitivity paper, arXiv:2007.08796 (2020-07).
- Only one counted item in the row; Gd neutron veto is listed as indeterminate and is not targeted.

## Documented alternative not adopted
- Gas-phase-only purification through hot getters, scaled up (more/larger getters, higher gas flow).

## adoption_status: built and operated
- XENONnT installed at LNGS 2020-2021 and operating; first WIMP results 2023 (PRL 131, 041003, arXiv:2303.14729). Liquid-phase purification system with LXe pump at ~2 L/min LXe reported electron lifetimes >10 ms (unverified exact figure; XENONnT detector paper arXiv:2402.10446).

## Physical handle for the conflict
- Hot (~400 C) zirconium getters chemisorb methane (CH4, CH3T); cold sorbents/getters/copper at ~170 K do not (no thermally activated dissociation of C-H at that temperature), and in a Xe solvent any physisorbent is saturated by Xe. LUX removed tritiated methane calibration source through its hot getter (LUX, PRD 93, 072009 (2016), arXiv:1512.03133).
