## Baseline as built: COSINE-100
- An array of 8 NaI(Tl) crystals, about 106 kg in total (61.3 kg used in the analysis after excluding 3 crystals with low light yield or high noise). Each crystal is read out by two 3-inch PMTs. The crystals sit in about 2,200 L of linear-alkylbenzene liquid scintillator that acts as an active veto, inside Cu, Pb and plastic-scintillator muon shielding.
- Site: Yangyang underground laboratory (Korea), about 700 m rock overburden (about 1,800 m w.e.).
- Physics goal: a model-independent test of the DAMA/LIBRA annual-modulation claim using the same target material, with a threshold of about 1-2 keVee. The background in 1-6 keV was about 2-4 counts/(kg day keV) (dru).
- Crystals: bought from Alpha Spectra Inc. (Grand Junction, Colorado, about 1,400 m altitude). The best crystals reached natK of about 16-20 ppb and 210Pb of about 0.7-3 mBq/kg (unverified, per crystal).
- Source: COSINE-100 Collaboration, "Initial performance of the COSINE-100 experiment", EPJC 78 (2018) 107, arXiv:1710.05299 (INSPIRE 1630770).

## Item targeted
For COSINE-200: growing ultra-pure NaI(Tl) crystals in-house (Kyropoulos grower at IBS/CUP, Korea) from NaI powder purified by recrystallisation, instead of buying commercial crystals. This is the single counted item in the row.

## Commitment document
arXiv:2004.06287v1 (2020-04): COSINE/KIMS subset, R&D on recrystallisation purification of NaI powder and in-house growth for COSINE-200. The row records a subset-of-collaboration authorship.

## Alternative not adopted
Continue buying commercial crystals from Alpha Spectra, as for COSINE-100. Other commercial routes exist in the field: RMD (Watertown, MA, near sea level) crystals grown from Merck Astro-Grade powder for SABRE, and Saint-Gobain for ANAIS-0/early DM-Ice.
