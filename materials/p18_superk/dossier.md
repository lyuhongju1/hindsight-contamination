# p18_superk dossier

- **Item:** dissolving gadolinium sulfate, Gd2(SO4)3, in the ultrapure water of a 50 kt water Cherenkov detector so that neutrons (e.g. from inverse beta decay) are tagged by capture on Gd (~8 MeV gamma cascade) instead of H (2.2 MeV gamma).
- **Dated commitment:** arXiv:1908.07551v1 (2019-08, D2, exact). D1: arXiv:2109.00360v1. The 2015 approval and the 2013-16 proceedings have no arXiv text, so the date is an upper bound.
- **Baseline:** Super-Kamiokande (2002-01).
- **Alternative not adopted:** GdCl3 (very soluble, but Cl- causes pitting corrosion of stainless steel). Non-Gd options (H capture only; other dissolved neutron absorbers) are possible survivors.
- **adoption_status:** built and operated since 2020 (SK-VI from 2020 at ~0.011% Gd, giving ~50% capture on Gd; SK-VII from 2022 at ~0.03% Gd, giving ~75%).
- **Outcome:** no robust conflict window (see notes.md). No twins were built, and kprobe.json = {}.
