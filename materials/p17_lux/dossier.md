# p17_lux dossier

## Baseline as built: LUX (2012)
- Dual-phase xenon TPC, ~370 kg LXe total (~250 kg active), read by 122 PMTs (top and bottom arrays),
  in a titanium cryostat. Goal: WIMP-nucleon spin-independent search via nuclear recoils.
- Shield: ~7.6 m diameter x 6.1 m tall water tank instrumented with 20 PMTs as a water Cherenkov muon veto;
  no scintillator neutron veto. Underground at 4850 ft (Davis Campus, SURF).
- Source: LUX Collaboration, NIM A 704 (2013) 111, arXiv:1211.3788 (INSPIRE 1202984, 2012-11).

## Item targeted
LZ outer detector: hermetic Gd-loaded LAB liquid-scintillator neutron veto (~17 t Gd-LS, ~0.1 wt% Gd,
in acrylic tanks around the cryostat, viewed by PMTs in the water tank). Single counted item; the
skin veto was not confirmed by both daters and is not counted.

## Commitment document (verified)
LZ Conceptual Design Report, arXiv:1509.02910 (2015-09); both daters exact.

## Alternatives documented, not adopted
- Boron-loaded (TMB) liquid scintillator veto.
- Water-Cherenkov-only veto (as in LUX).

## adoption_status
built and operated -- the LZ outer detector operated in the first LZ science run
