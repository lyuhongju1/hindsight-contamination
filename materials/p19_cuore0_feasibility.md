# p19_cuore0 feasibility (lesson 5): CUORE-0 -> CUPID, item A = Li2(100)MoO4 + light detectors

VERDICT: NO WINDOW (item A and item B).

- Pile-up of 2nu-bb random coincidences: rate per 280 g crystal (97% 100Mo, T1/2 7.1e18 yr) is A ~ 2.8e-3 Hz. ZnSe (440 g, 82Se, 8.7e19 yr) gives 4.3e-4 Hz; natural TeO2 (750 g, 130Te, 7.9e20 yr) gives 2.7e-5 Hz. Background index b ~ tau*A^2*f/M with f ~ 1e-4 /keV (order-of-magnitude spectral density of two-event sums at Q, consistent with Chernyak et al. 2012 and the CUPID CDR). This gives b ~ 9e-5 at tau = 1 ms, 4.6e-5 at 0.5 ms and 1.5e-5 at 0.17 ms (NTL-boosted light detector) in counts/(keV kg yr), against a CUPID-like 1e-4 target. Because b/M scales as M*tau, halving the crystal mass or using a faster or NTL-amplified light detector with pulse-shape pile-up rejection always restores the margin. These are routine design tweaks (lesson 4) or add-ons (lesson 3). Forbidding them would need a stated crystal mass or resolving time, which is a design fiat and not physics. A requirement strict enough to defeat Li2MoO4 robustly under x0.5-2 (it must also beat tau x0.5 and M x0.5) would need b far below 1e-5. ZnSe is only about 6.6x lower in A, about 40x lower in A^2/M, so a few-x window exists in principle. That window closes as soon as tau or M is varied. Not robust. NO WINDOW.
- Natural 2nu-bb rate (irreducible tail into the ROI): for a 2nu-bb spectrum with Q = 3034 keV, the fraction above Q - ~5 keV scales as (dE/Q)^6, about 1e-12 or less. This is negligible compared with any background index at FWHM ~5-7 keV, so the tail does not separate the crystals. NO WINDOW.
- Crystal radiopurity: Li2MoO4 measures < few uBq/kg in 228Th and 226Ra (CUPID-Mo/CROSS). This equals or beats ZnSe (CUPID-0 had tens of uBq/kg plus 82Se-chain issues) and CdWO4, and Q = 3034 keV sits above the 2615 keV 208Tl line. A purity requirement favours the item. NO WINDOW.
- Energy resolution: Li2MoO4 is about 5-7 keV FWHM at 2.6-3.0 MeV (CUPID-Mo reports 7.4 keV FWHM at 3034 keV; CROSS/CUPID R&D about 5-6 keV FWHM). TeO2 is about 5-8 keV FWHM (CUORE-0 about 5 keV FWHM at 2615; CUORE about 7.8 keV FWHM). ZnSe is about 20 keV FWHM (CUPID-0, at Qbb). The only survivor with better resolution (TeO2) differs by at most about 1.3x, inside x0.5-2. Any requirement strict enough to defeat Li2MoO4 also defeats TeO2-Cherenkov. NO WINDOW.
- Item B (NTD-Ge light detectors): the only physical lever is timing or noise (ms rise time). NTL amplification on the same Ge disk, or a smaller sensor, is a routine add-on (lessons 3 and 4). Light yield needed for alpha rejection in Li2MoO4 is easily met. NO WINDOW.

Survivors are not needed because no route separates the item robustly.
