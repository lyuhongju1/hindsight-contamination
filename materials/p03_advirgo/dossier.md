# Dossier: p03_advirgo

## Baseline as built
- Advanced Virgo (AdV), Cascina, Italy (EGO). Technical Design Report VIR-0128A-12 (2012); overview paper F. Acernese et al., CQG 32, 024001 (2015) (arXiv:1408.3978; INSPIRE 1311317, 2014-08).
- Dual-recycled Michelson with 3 km Fabry-Perot arms, 1064 nm, ~42 kg fused-silica test masses, monolithic suspensions on superattenuators; design BNS range ~ 130-140 Mpc at full power.
- O3 (2019-20): frequency-independent squeezed vacuum injected (~3 dB at high frequency). Source: Acernese et al., PRL 123, 231108 (2019).

## Targeted item
- AdV+ (Phase I, for O4): **frequency-dependent squeezing** produced by reflecting the squeezed vacuum off a detuned ~285 m high-finesse filter cavity, so that squeezing reduces shot noise at high frequency and radiation-pressure noise at low frequency.
- Only one counted item in the row; it is the one used.

## Commitment document (from SELECTED_PAIRS row 3)
- arXiv:1304.0670v7 (Living Reviews in Relativity, "Prospects for observing and localizing GW transients with Advanced LIGO, Advanced Virgo and KAGRA"), v7 dated 2019-09; the quote committing Virgo to FDS is absent in v6 (version-aware dating).

## Alternatives documented but not adopted
- Frequency-independent squeezing (as used in O3): degrades low-frequency sensitivity through extra radiation-pressure noise once arm power rises.
- EPR / conditional squeezing (Ma et al., Nature Phys. 13, 776 (2017); demonstrated on tabletop, Suedbeck et al. Nature Photon. 2020, Yap et al. 2020): uses the interferometer itself as the filter for a detuned idler beam, avoiding an external filter cavity, at the cost of a second readout and a conditional (~3 dB at most for low source squeezing) penalty.
