# Dossier: p09_nemo3

## Baseline as built (NEMO-3, Modane, data 2003-2011)
- Tracker-calorimeter double-beta experiment: thin source foils (~10 kg of 100Mo, 82Se, ...) between two drift-cell (Geiger-mode) tracking volumes in a ~25 G solenoidal field; calorimeter walls of 1940 plastic scintillator blocks read by 3-in. and 5-in. low-radioactivity PMTs (mostly through light guides). Energy resolution ~14-17% FWHM at 1 MeV, timing ~250 ps sigma at 1 MeV. Source: NEMO-3 technical paper, Arnold et al., NIM A 536 (2005) 79 [hep-ex/0402... commitment row: 645078, 2004-02].
- Physics goal: 2nu-beta-beta measurements and 0nu-beta-beta search with full event topology (individual electron energies, angles, timing).

## Targeted item
Calorimeter optical modules using 8-in. high-QE PMTs (Hamamatsu R5912-MOD) coupled directly to large polystyrene scintillator blocks (~256 x 256 x 194 mm), aiming at ~7-8% FWHM at 1 MeV (SuperNEMO). Only one counted item in the row.

## Commitment document
arXiv:1707.06823v1 (2017-07), Barabash et al., "Calorimeter development for the SuperNEMO double beta decay experiment", NIM A 868 (2017) 98. Abstract (fetched 2026-11-06): goal ~7% FWHM/sqrt(E) with large plastic blocks + PMTs; final design meets it. Row notes possibly earlier non-arXiv commitment.

## Alternatives documented, not adopted
5-in. PMTs (used only in X-walls / gamma veto of the demonstrator, not the main wall), 10-in. and 11-in. PMTs, PVT scintillator (polystyrene adopted).

## adoption_status
**built and operated** - the SuperNEMO Demonstrator main calorimeter walls (520 8-in. modules) were installed at Modane and commissioned/taking data from ~2019-2023 onward. Source: SuperNEMO collaboration demonstrator papers (from memory; not re-fetched here). Status classification confident; exact dates unverified.
