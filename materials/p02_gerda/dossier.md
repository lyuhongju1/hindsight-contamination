# Dossier: p02_gerda

## Baseline as built
GERDA Phase II (INSPIRE 1634658, 2017-11): ~35 kg of enriched 76Ge (coaxial + BEGe) operated bare in ~64 m^3 of
atmospheric liquid argon (LAr) in a stainless cryostat inside a water Cherenkov muon veto. LAr scintillation readout
(PMTs + fibres) serves as an active veto; pulse-shape discrimination (PSD) on Ge signals. Detector strings are
surrounded by transparent nylon "mini-shrouds" (TPB-coated) to keep 42K ions away from detector surfaces.
Physics goal: search for 0vbb decay of 76Ge (Q_bb = 2039 keV) with background ~1e-3 cts/(keV kg yr).
Source: GERDA Phase II upgrade/first-results papers (Nature 544 (2017) 47; EPJC 78 (2018) 388) -- from memory, not refetched.

## Targeted item
Underground-sourced argon (UAr), low in 42Ar, for the LAr around the detector array of the successor (LEGEND-1000).
Chosen over the second counted item (ASIC front end) because the argon choice has a clean physical background
mechanism: 42Ar (T1/2 ~ 33 y) -> 42K (T1/2 = 12.36 h, beta endpoint 3.525 MeV > Q_bb, ~82% ground-state branch,
i.e. no accompanying gamma for the LAr veto). 42K+ ions drift in the detectors' fields and collect on surfaces.
UAr removes the source; atmospheric 42Ar is cosmogenic, absent in argon shielded underground.

## Commitment document
LEGEND-1000 Preconceptual Design Report, arXiv:2107.11462 (2021-07): UAr in the re-entrant inner volumes holding the
Ge strings, atmospheric LAr in the outer cryostat.

## Documented alternative not adopted
Atmospheric LAr with 42K mitigation by mini-shrouds / encapsulation of the detectors (GERDA Phase II and LEGEND-200
practice; Lubashevskiy et al., EPJC 78 (2018) 15, arXiv:1708.00226 -- mitigation studies in LArGe).
