## Notes for the sender

- **Recruit panelists who are not authors on the baseline or upgrade papers of the pairs they would rate.** Collaboration members know the history too well for blind rating. Check them against the author lists before inviting.
- **Track the invitation count, acceptances and refusals** for each field. The paper reports the response rate.
- **Do not mention "contamination", "language models proposing", or specific experiments** before participation. Saying the study is about evaluating AI on scientific design tasks is honest and enough. The full purpose is given in the debrief.
