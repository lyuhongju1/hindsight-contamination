## BASELINE q46 1602572: Performance of the EDELWEISS-III experiment (2017-06-04)
Upgrade program: EDELWEISS-SubGeV (followed by CRYOSEL), run by the same collaboration at LSM.
items:
 1. NbSi thin-film transition-edge sensor (TES) as the phonon sensor of the Ge heat-and-ionization bolometer, replacing the NTD-Ge thermistor | commitment 2022-03 (arXiv:2203.03993, EDELWEISS collaboration underground DM search with a 200 g Ge + NbSi TES detector at 66 V) | gap 57 months | alternative not adopted: NTD-Ge thermistor, i.e. thermal-phonon sensing. It was used in EDELWEISS-III and in the 33.4 g sub-MeV detector, and the SubGeV plan stated in arXiv:2003.01046 was still to operate "the NTD sensor at lower temperature". Ruled out: arXiv:1707.04308 and arXiv:1901.03588 contain no mention of NbSi/TES. | conflict idea: a NbSi TES is a superconducting-transition (athermal phonon) sensor with a narrow operating window. A stated requirement for linear, unsaturated response over a wide energy range (keV-MeV calibration lines and backgrounds) or for operation at a fixed bath temperature away from the film's Tc would break the TES, while the semiconducting NTD thermistor (smooth R(T), large dynamic range) survives.
verdict: ELIGIBLE (item 1)
Notes: the item is a different sensor physics (superconducting-transition film vs. hopping-conduction NTD semiconductor), not readout. HEMT cold preamplifiers replacing JFETs are another SubGeV item, but they are readout/electronics, so I deliberately did not count them. adoption_status: built and operated underground (2203.03993); NbSi sensors are also central to CRYOSEL (arXiv:2303.02067). Caveat on "chosen plan" (A1 addendum 3): the 2022 paper presents an operated detector of the program rather than a declaration of the program baseline. Possible slightly earlier date: proceedings arXiv:2112.05467 (2021-12), not checked. This is immaterial for I2/I6.
