## BASELINE q59 796887: The CMS Experiment at the CERN LHC (2008-08)
items: none datable
verdict: **NOT ELIGIBLE** (dating, §6: no arXiv full text for the first commitment)
- An INSPIRE search of `rn CMS-TDR*` turned up every CMS upgrade commitment: Phase-1 Pixel TDR (CERN-LHCC-2012-016), Phase-1 HCAL TDR (CERN-LHCC-2012-015), L1 Trigger TDR (2013-011), Phase-II Technical Proposal (CERN-LHCC-2015-010), GEM TDR (2015-012), and the Phase-2 Tracker, Muon, Endcap Calorimeter/HGCAL, MTD and L1 TDRs (2017 to 2020). None of them has an arXiv eprint.
- The arXiv items in the candidate list (for example 1101.5977, 1110.1173, 1211.3939 and 1410.1399) are R&D studies or proceedings by subgroups, written after or alongside the TDRs. None is the first collaboration commitment.
- Physically strong items exist but cannot be dated by the verifier: HCAL HPD to SiPM (constraint: 3.8 T field and HPD discharge), HGCAL silicon/scintillator sampling calorimeter (constraint: endcap fluence up to ~1e16 neq/cm2), and the 4-layer Phase-1 pixel with CO2 cooling.

items:
 1. Upgrade strip redesign: 2 cm thick, 5 cm wide, 120 cm long polystyrene strips with 8 WLS-fibre grooves on top and bottom and SiPM readout at both ends, with no PMTs (baseline: 1x4x100 cm strips, 3 fibres, SiPM+PMT) | commitment 2021-12 | gap 66 months | alternative not adopted: cube-segmented PVT with 6LiF:ZnS and fibre/MPPC readout (SoLid, arXiv:1703.01683), or segmented 6Li-doped liquid scintillator (PROSPECT, arXiv:1808.00097) | conflict idea: photostatistics for 12% at 1 MeV with a fixed channel count and a fixed 1.2 m frame volume
 2. Gd for neutron capture moved out of the strip reflective coating into a separate Gd2O3-loaded polyethylene film (250 um) between strip layers | commitment 2021-12 | gap 66 months | alternative not adopted: 6Li capture (6LiF:ZnS screens, SoLid arXiv:1703.01683; 6Li-loaded LS, PROSPECT arXiv:1808.00097), or keeping the Gd-loaded coating | conflict idea: containment of the 8 MeV Gd gamma cascade compared with the localized alpha+triton from 6Li; neutron capture time and position

Caveats:
- The commitment paper (Alekseev, Danilov, Svirida et al.) is by the core DANSS group but is not signed "DANSS Collaboration". It describes "the planned upgrade" with a single chosen design.
- A non-arXiv proceeding, "DANSS experiment: current status and future plans" (INSPIRE, 2020-12), could predate it by about 12 months. That would not change I2 or I6.
- Item 1 is partly a geometry and readout change, so item 2 is the more physical of the two.

## BASELINE q61 747655: The COMPASS experiment at CERN (2007-01)
items: none qualifying
verdict: **NOT ELIGIBLE** (dating §6 and A1 addendum 3)
- COMPASS-II Proposal (CERN-SPSC-2010-014; DVCS recoil detector CAMERA, ECAL0) has no arXiv full text.
- RICH-1 hybrid THGEM+Micromegas CsI photon detectors: the first commitments are 2012 to 2016 group proceedings, none of them on arXiv. The arXiv papers (1804.05765 and later) appeared after installation in 2016.
- AMBER LoI (arXiv:1808.00848, the successor):
  - The best physical item, a high-pressure H2 active TPC target for mu-p elastic scattering, is listed as "high-pressure active TPC target (similar to A2 at MAMI) or hydrogen tube surrounded by SciFis". That is an open X-or-Y option, so it fails A1 addendum 3.
  - The MWPC replacement is "based on large-area GEM or Micromegas technology". Both technologies were already in the 2007 baseline, and the choice is also open.
  - The PixelGEMs are a scale-up.
  - The AMBER Phase-1 proposal (2019) has no arXiv full text.
