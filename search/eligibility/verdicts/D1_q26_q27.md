## BASELINE q27 1449116 - CUORE-0 detector: design, construction and operation (2016-04-19)
Upgrade program: CUPID (declared successor of CUORE, same cryostat). First adoption document: CUPID pre-CDR, arXiv:1907.09376 (2019-07-22).
The earlier CUPID R&D documents (arXiv:1504.03599, 2015; 1504.03612) list several open options (TeO2+Cherenkov, ZnSe, Li2MoO4, ...), so under A1 addendum 3 they are not commitments, and they also precede the baseline.
items:
 1. Li2(100)MoO4 scintillating bolometer crystals (100Mo, Qbb = 3034 keV) instead of TeO2 | commitment 2019-07 | gap 39 months | alternative not adopted: enriched 130TeO2 with Cherenkov-light PID (the pre-CDR keeps it as "a possible alternative", Sec. 10, arXiv:1907.09376) and ZnSe/82Se (CUPID-0, arXiv:1802.07791) | conflict idea: Q-value relative to the 2615 keV 208Tl gamma line versus the light yield needed for alpha rejection (Cherenkov light ~10x weaker than Li2MoO4 scintillation)
 2. Bolometric Ge-wafer light detectors with NTD readout (absent in CUORE-0) | commitment 2019-07 | gap 39 months | alternative not adopted: TES or MKID light detectors (pre-CDR Sec. 5.4.2 and 5.4.3, arXiv:1907.09376) | conflict idea: slow NTD rise time versus 100Mo 2vbb pile-up rejection (timing resolution), set against compatibility with CUORE wiring
verdict: ELIGIBLE (items 1, 2). Item 1 is preferred because its alternative is a physically different approach (a different isotope or crystal and a different light mechanism).
Residual risk: a CUPID collaboration paper committing to Li2MoO4 before 2018-04-19 would break I2. None was found, but the search was not exhaustive.
