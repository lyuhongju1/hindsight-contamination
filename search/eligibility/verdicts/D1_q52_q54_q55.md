# D1 verdicts: q52, q54, q55

Dater D1. Claims are in `claims/D1_q52_q54_q55.json` and the verifier output is in `claims/D1_q52_q54_q55_verified.json`. All 4 claims are VERIFIED with exact quote matches.

```
BASELINE q52 786324 — The Magnetized Steel and Scintillator Calorimeters of the MINOS Experiment (2008-05)
items: none
verdict: NOT ELIGIBLE (I4 and I3 in substance)
```
- **Program:** MINOS+ ran the unchanged MINOS detectors in the higher-energy NuMI beam. The collaboration declared no successor detector. NOvA is a different collaboration.
- **What changed:** The only hardware absent from the baseline is the DAQ replacement: front-end readout controllers, the event builder and a Gigabit-Ethernet TCP/IP farm (arXiv:1506.02197; FERMILAB-CONF-14-503).
- **Why it fails:**
  - The replacement was driven by "obsolescence, reliability problems, and data throughput limitations".
  - No not-adopted alternative is documented at commitment, so I4 fails.
  - It is a DAQ/computing choice, not a physical one.
  - The document is a proceedings by a DAQ subgroup, not a collaboration commitment.
- **Candidate list:** The other candidate documents (IDS-NF, nuSTORM, Mu2e, ILC, Auger, JSNS2, DUNE, LEGEND) are other programs citing MINOS. They are not this program.

- **Dating caveat:** The earliest arXiv-hosted collaboration document is the 2013 JINST paper (arXiv:1302.6092). The true first commitment, the 2002 TDR, is not on arXiv. Korotkov and Nowak (hep-ex/0108077) is a two-author study, not a collaboration commitment.
- **Adoption:** The detector was built, installed in late 2005 and operated 2006–07.

- **Item 1** is the stronger item: the shielding medium differs (water versus high-Z/hydrogenous layers).
- **Item 2 caveat:** 3-inch R11410 tubes were already in the baseline's bottom array. The new element is their use in the top array.
- **Excluded item:** The stainless-steel cryostat was not counted, because the baseline already used a stainless-steel inner vessel.
- **Commitment document:** arXiv:1806.02229, the PandaX-4T sensitivity paper by the collaboration. The plan is stated as the chosen design ("will be placed"), so A1 addendum 3 is met.
- **Adoption:** Both items were built.
