# D2 verdict: q60 1468455, DANSS: Detector of the reactor AntiNeutrino based on Solid Scintillator (baseline 2016-06-09)

Upgrade programme: the DANSS detector upgrade (new 120 cm strips, 60 layers x 24 strips), a DANSS Collaboration paper, arXiv:2112.04973 (v1 2021-12-09). INSPIRE title and full-text searches found no earlier DANSS upgrade document. Any commitment after 2018-06 would still pass I2.

items:
 1. Gd moved out of the strip's reflective coating into a separate Gd2O3-loaded polyethylene film (250 um) between strip layers | commitment 2021-12 | gap 66 months | alternative not adopted: keep Gd2O3 in the TiO2 co-extruded strip coating, as in the baseline (arXiv:1606.02896); another option was 6LiF:ZnS(Ag) neutron-capture screens (SoLid, arXiv:1806.02461) | conflict idea: neutron capture time and efficiency vs. inert-layer thickness; the coating thickness (100-300 um, poorly controlled) costs light yield and energy resolution.
 2. Double-sided SiPM-only readout of 8 WLS fibres per strip (4 SiPMs per end, alternating fibres), with the vacuum PMTs removed | commitment 2021-12 | gap 66 months | alternative not adopted: single-ended readout with a mirrored (silver-painted) far fibre end plus a hybrid SiPM + bundled PMT readout, as in the baseline (arXiv:1606.02896) | conflict idea: longitudinal position from the end-to-end time difference for isolated hits (about 12 cm); light-yield attenuation over 1.2 m strips; the channel count is fixed by funding.

verdict: ELIGIBLE (items 1, 2)

Note: the paper describes a tested design "for the DANSS upgrade" and uses commitment language for the final design ("will be wired together in the final design"). I read it as the programme's chosen plan, not one of several open options (A1 addendum 3). The adoption_status covariate is left for the materials stage.
