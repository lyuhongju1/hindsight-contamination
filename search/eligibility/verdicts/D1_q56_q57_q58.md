# D1 eligibility verdicts: q56, q57, q58 (Study 2)

## BASELINE q56 1384774: The NuMI Neutrino Beam (baseline date 2015-07-23)
Upgrade programme: NuMI has no collaboration and no collaboration-level upgrade document after 2015 on arXiv (post-2015 NuMI 1 MW target work is in accelerator proceedings only). The only candidate is the LBNF beamline (DUNE TDR), a separate new facility built on NuMI experience, not a declared successor of a NuMI collaboration.

## BASELINE q57 1233527: The BABAR Detector: Upgrades, Operation and Performance (baseline date 2013-05-15)
Upgrade programme: BaBar stopped taking data in 2008. The only BaBar-lineage successor is SuperB; its TDR (arXiv:1306.5655, 2013-06-24) comes 1 month after the baseline, and SuperB was cancelled (INFN funding withdrawn, late 2012), so no later commitment exists. Belle II is a different collaboration (excluded under the successor rule). FCC-ee is not BaBar. An INSPIRE search for BaBar upgrade or design documents after 2013 finds only physics talks.
verdict: NOT ELIGIBLE (I2: the only successor commitment, the SuperB TDR, is dated 2013-06, a gap of 1 month, under 24; there is no later programme. SuperB is also arguably not the same collaboration.)

## BASELINE q58 1235030: Design, Calibration, and Performance of the MINERvA Detector (baseline date 2013-05-22)
Upgrade programme: none. MINERvA ran in the NuMI LE and ME beams until 2019 with no new hardware choices after the baseline. The candidate documents (PINGU, LBNF/DUNE, DUNE ND CDR, T2K ND280 upgrade, pyramid muography) belong to other collaborations. Reuse of MINERvA planes in the DUNE 2x2 demonstrator is a DUNE choice, not a MINERvA one. An INSPIRE search (MINERvA collaboration, detector/design titles, after 2015) returns only physics analyses.
verdict: NOT ELIGIBLE (item definition / I3: there is no upgrade programme or declared successor of the MINERvA collaboration that adopts a hardware choice 24 or more months after the baseline).
