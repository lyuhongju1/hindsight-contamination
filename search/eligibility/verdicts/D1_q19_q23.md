# D1 eligibility verdicts: q19 LUX, q23 Super-Kamiokande

Dater D1, 2026-11-06. Worked independently; no other dater's claims or verdicts were read. Web fetches used: 7 of 8 (arXiv PDFs 1310.8214 [wrong document, LUX first results], 1110.0103, 1509.02910, 1805.04163, 1502.05199, 2109.00360, plus one INSPIRE search). A1 addenda applied: "adopted" means the collaboration's chosen plan, commitment <= 2023-06-30, gap >= 24 months.

Notes, q19:
- "After LUX: The LZ Program" (arXiv:1110.0103, 2011-10, INSPIRE tags it as LZ) predates the baseline. It already proposes the 7.6 cm R11410 PMTs, the Ti cryostat and an organic scintillator volume that would probably run at LXe temperature. Those items therefore fail I2 (the commitment comes before the baseline). The same paper has no Gd loading and no instrumented skin; I grepped it for "gadolin" and "skin" and found neither. The CDR is the first collaboration document I found with either one.
- Residual risk: LZ members' conference proceedings from 2013-14 might mention GdLS or the skin. Snowmass 2013 (Cushman et al., arXiv:1310.8327) is a community summary, not a collaboration document. Even a 2013 date would move item 1 to roughly 11 months, so the gap is the weak point if such a document turns up.
- The CDR presents GdLS and the skin as the design ("will be dissolved", "is observed by a sparse PMT array"), not as one of several open options. It meets A1 addendum 3.

Notes, q23:
- Hyper-K 2015 (arXiv:1502.05199) keeps the R3600 as baseline and lists box-and-line and HPD as "possible alternative options". It is not a commitment under A1 addendum 3. The Design Report is the first document to adopt the R12860; I checked only the 2018 arXiv version. The 2016 KEK preprint has no arXiv full text, so the verifier date is an upper bound. Either date passes I2 and I6.
- SK-Gd: the collaboration decided in 2015, but the first SK collaboration paper on arXiv I found that states the plan is the 2021 loading paper, so 2021-09 is an upper bound. Every SK collaboration document is at least 24 months after 2002-01. Beacom & Vagins (2003) is a phenomenology proposal from outside the collaboration, so it does not set the commitment date. The item meets I2 and I6 whatever the exact date.
- Lineage: Hyper-K and SK-Gd both belong to the Super-K lineage, which no earlier selection has entered.
