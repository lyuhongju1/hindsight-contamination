BASELINE q37 895584 — ANTARES: the first undersea neutrino telescope (2011-04)
programme: KM3NeT (ARCA/ORCA; KM3NeT 2.0 LoI arXiv:1601.07459)
items: none qualifying
 - multi-PMT digital optical module (31 x 3-inch PMTs instead of one 10-inch PMT): fails I2. It was the KM3NeT design in the 2010 TDR (and an option in the 2008 CDR), before the baseline. In any case, the first prototype DOM was deployed on the ANTARES instrumentation line in April 2013, so the commitment must predate baseline + 24 months (2013-04).
 - flexible-string detection unit (instead of the NEMO tower): adopted about 2012 for prototypes deployed 2013-14, so it fails I2. It is also a mechanical/engineering choice (I3/guidance).
 - KM3NeT 2.0 (2016) adds layouts and spacing (ORCA density), which are geometry/scale-up, not new hardware (I3).
verdict: NOT ELIGIBLE (I2: the successor's distinctive hardware was committed before or within 24 months of the baseline)
caveat: not checked against the TDR full text (the km3net.org TDR URL returned HTML; no arXiv version). The I2 failure is inferred from the 2010 TDR and the April 2013 prototype deployment. Also, whether KM3NeT counts as ANTARES's "declared successor of the same collaboration" is arguable.
