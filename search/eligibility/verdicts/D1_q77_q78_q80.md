Dater D1. Baseline dates "2003" are taken as 2003-01. Verifier: all 3 claims VERIFIED (exact).

## BASELINE q77 621253 — The CEBAF Large Acceptance Spectrometer (CLAS)
Program: JLab 12 GeV upgrade, CLAS -> CLAS12 (same Hall B collaboration).
items:
 1. PCAL: a 5-6 X0 lead-scintillator preshower calorimeter added in front of the existing EC | commitment 2008-10 (arXiv:0810.4718) | gap 69 months | alternative not adopted: finer-granularity PbWO4 crystal calorimetry over the full EC acceptance, the technology of the CLAS DVCS inner calorimeter (Girod et al., PRL 100 (2008) 162002), which CLAS12 used only below 6 deg (0810.4718); or keeping the EC alone (Amarian et al., NIM A460 (2001) 239) | conflict idea: separating the two photons of a pi0 at 9-10 GeV/c, given the opening angle and the transverse shower size at the EC distance.
 Rejected: 5 T central solenoid replacing the CLAS mini-torus to sweep out Moller electrons. This is a strong physics item (alternative: the mini-torus described in the baseline paper), but the central solenoid was most likely already in the public 12 GeV concept (2001 white paper and 2004 pCDR, not on arXiv), so I2 probably fails. The arXiv:0711.4741 mention (2007-11) is not the first commitment.
verdict: ELIGIBLE (item 1), with caveats for the coordinator:
 (a) 0810.4718 is a single-author overview by V. Burkert (JLab Hall B leader) of the approved CLAS12 design. It is not a formal collaboration document.
 (b) Earlier non-arXiv commitments probably exist: the 12 GeV CDR (2005) and the PCAL TDR (2007). They do not break I2 unless PCAL already appears in the 2004 pCDR, which I did not check.
 (c) The constraint is real physics (shower separation), but PCAL is partly a granularity upgrade, so the alternative is somewhat weak.
 0711.4741 has no PCAL mention.

## BASELINE q78 627886 — PHENIX detector overview
Program: sPHENIX. The commitment document is arXiv:1207.6378, "An Upgrade Concept from the PHENIX Collaboration". Its executive summary says: "The PHENIX collaboration presents in this document a proposal for a major upgrade to the PHENIX detector at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider."
Successor rule (as for KM3NeT/ANTARES): the formal sPHENIX collaboration came later and is a new collaboration. Unlike KM3NeT, however, the first commitment was made by the baseline collaboration itself, as an upgrade of the baseline detector. So I count it as "a declared successor detector of the same collaboration". If the coordinator applies the KM3NeT ruling strictly, i.e. a new collaboration is out regardless of who made the commitment, this baseline becomes NOT ELIGIBLE.
PHENIX's own upgrades, not used: VTX (barrel silicon, stripixel and pixel), FVTX, HBD, MPC and the muon-trigger RPCs. VTX and HBD were in the PHENIX upgrade plans and proposals of about 2003-2004, so I2 likely fails or is borderline. The arXiv items (nucl-ex/0703041, 0801.2947) are not the first commitments. The FVTX/RPC proposals of about 2006-2007 are not on arXiv, and the RPCs are a trigger/readout choice.
items:
 1. Compact tungsten-scintillating-fibre sampling EMCal (SiPM/APD readout), replacing the PHENIX PbSc/PbGl central-arm EMCal concept | commitment 2012-07 | gap 114 months | alternative not adopted: the ATLAS-style lead/LAr accordion (the design the doc says it descends from, its ref. [96]); the PHENIX PbSc shashlik and PbGl calorimeters (Aphecetche et al., NIM A499 (2003) 521) | conflict idea: a small Moliere radius and short X0 within a thin radial slot just outside the coil, working in the magnet's fringe field.
 2. New thin superconducting solenoid (<1 X0, radial thickness <20 cm) with the EMCal outside the coil, replacing the PHENIX axial-field central magnet | commitment 2012-07 | gap 114 months | alternative not adopted: re-using existing solenoids (CDF, BaBar, CLEO, D0; the doc's Table 3.1 rejects them as too large, too massive or unavailable) | conflict idea: material budget in front of the EMCal versus bore radius and field. Note: this choice was later reversed, since sPHENIX adopted the BaBar magnet with the EMCal inside it. Record adoption_status = not built (A1 addendum 3).
verdict: ELIGIBLE (items 1, 2), conditional on the successor ruling above. Item 1 preferred.

## BASELINE q80 629267 — The PHOBOS detector at RHIC
The candidate file is empty. An INSPIRE query found 70 documents citing the baseline that match upgrade, proposal or the PHOBOS collaboration; all of them are physics or analysis papers. PHOBOS ended data-taking in 2005 with no upgrade program and no successor detector. Its minor run-period additions (trigger and TOF changes) were trigger/readout choices made within 24 months of the baseline and are not on arXiv.
verdict: NOT ELIGIBLE (item definition/I3, with I2 and I4 unfulfillable): there is no upgrade program and no declared successor. No claims.
