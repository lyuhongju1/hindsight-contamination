## BASELINE q49 1084119: The Tunka-133 EAS Cherenkov light array: status of 2011 (2012-01)
Candidate file was empty, so I searched INSPIRE myself.
Upgrade lineage: Tunka-Rex (radio), Tunka-Grande (scintillators), TAIGA-HiSCORE (wide-angle timing Cherenkov), TAIGA-IACT (imaging).
- Tunka-Rex radio antennas: the collaboration's first public documents are 2012-2013 (deployed in 2012; arXiv:1308.0917, 2013-08), less than 24 months after 2012-01. **Fails I2.**
- HiSCORE non-imaging stations: the concept was public in 2011 (arXiv:1108.5880, with Tunka co-authors), before the baseline date. **Fails I2.**
- TAIGA-IACT (imaging telescopes added to the timing array) and Tunka-Grande (scintillator muon/charged-particle array): these pass I2 on date (first commitments are about 2014-09 and 2015-04: INSPIRE 1318563, 1359620, 1484037), but none of those earliest documents has an arXiv full text. The earliest arXiv document I found for IACT is arXiv:1702.07768 (2017-02), which is not the first commitment. **Cannot be dated under the brief's arXiv requirement.**
If a later arXiv document is acceptable as the dating document: IACT vs. a timing-only (HiSCORE) array; constraint hint: if gamma/hadron separation is needed below about 30 TeV with a limited duty cycle, a pointed imaging telescope with a narrow field of view fails sky-survey coverage, while a wide-angle timing array survives.
**verdict: NOT ELIGIBLE (I2 for radio and HiSCORE; the remaining items have no datable arXiv commitment).**

## BASELINE q50 657665: Basic Instrumentation for Hall A at Jefferson Lab (2004)
Upgrade program: Hall A 12 GeV equipment, SoLID (its base equipment includes "Requisite Hall A infrastructure").
1. MRPC (multi-gap resistive plate chamber) time-of-flight for hadron PID | commitment 2014-09 (arXiv:1409.7741) | gap > 120 months | alternative not adopted: plastic-scintillator TOF bars with PMTs (the baseline's Hall A scintillator trigger planes, Alcorn et al., NIM A 522 (2004) 294) | constraint hint: float-glass MRPC rate capability is limited to about 1 kHz/cm²; at higher stated hit rates its efficiency collapses, while fast scintillator survives.
2. GEM tracking detectors | commitment 2014-09 (arXiv:1409.7741) | gap > 120 months | alternative not adopted: wire drift chambers (Hall A VDCs, same baseline NIM paper) | constraint hint: if the momentum resolution at low momentum is dominated by multiple scattering and requires a material budget per plane below about 0.2% X0, multi-foil triple-GEMs fail, while low-mass drift chambers survive (provided rates are moderate).
Caveats: the SoLID pre-CDR (INSPIRE 1241635, 2013/2014, no arXiv) and the approved 2010 SoLID proposals probably name GEMs and MRPC earlier. The arXiv date is therefore an upper bound on the commitment date, and I2 and I6 hold either way. It is arguable whether SoLID counts as the "upgrade program" of the Hall A baseline. GEM vs. drift chamber is the weaker of the two physics contrasts.
**verdict: ELIGIBLE (items 1, 2)**, item 1 preferred.

## BASELINE q51 705112: The DRIFT-II dark matter detector: Design and commissioning (2005)
Upgrade program: DRIFT-IId/IIe gas and cathode upgrades. (The only candidate document, LZ CDR, is not this lineage.)
1. CF4 admixture (30:10 Torr CS2:CF4) for a 19F spin-dependent target | commitment 2010-10 (arXiv:1010.3027; no CF4 in arXiv:0903.0326, 2009) | gap about 69 months | alternative not adopted: pure CF4 electron-drift TPC (DMTPC, Ahlen et al., PLB 695 (2011) 124) | constraint hint: if the 19F target mass per volume must exceed what 10 Torr CF4 gives at fixed total pressure and gain, the CS2-dominated mixture fails, while a higher-pressure pure-CF4 fill survives (when the drift length is short enough that electron diffusion is tolerable).
2. O2 additive (1 Torr) producing minority carriers for z-fiducialisation | commitment 2014-10 (arXiv:1410.7821) | gap about 117 months | alternative not adopted: z from transverse diffusion width of the track (Lewis et al., NIM A 789 (2015) 81), or rejection by cathode material only (thin aluminised-mylar cathode, arXiv:1502.03535) | constraint hint: if the arrival-time separation of the minority peaks is unresolvable at the stated short drift distance or low gas pressure, or if O2 attachment losses are forbidden, minority-carrier fiducialisation fails, while diffusion-based z survives.
Caveat: the minority carriers were discovered in an R&D paper (Snowden-Ifft 2014, INSPIRE 1291855, no arXiv), which I do not count as a commitment.
**verdict: ELIGIBLE (items 1, 2)**
